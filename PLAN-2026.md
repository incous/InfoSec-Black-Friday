## Part 1: re-check last year's deals

Goal: each 2025 entry ends up as **carry over**, **updated**, or **dropped**.

1. Extract entries into a worklist (vendor, URL, category, 2025 code, 2025 end date).
2. Visit each URL and classify it:
   - **Live 2026 deal:** copy the entry into `README.md`. Update the code, discount, dates and source link. Remove `:new:` and rocket flags unless re-confirmed.
   - **Announced / waitlist:** add it only with a clear "announced / waitlist" note.
   - **No 2026 deal found:** leave it out, and record that it was checked.
   - **Dead link, redirect to a different company, or domain change:** drop it and note why.
3. Do not reuse 2025 codes (`...2025`) without confirming that they still work. Most vendors rename them.
4. Apply the 2025 vetting rules: infosec related, no one-time or gated deals, no affiliate links, and flag claims above 90% off.
5. Track results in a table (vendor | status | checked on | source) so a later pass can resume.

Order of work: Tools, then Practical Labs, then Online Courses (largest group), then Hardware, Services and the rest. Tools and labs tend to announce early.

## Part 2: find new infosec deals

Sources, roughly in order of yield:

- **Vendor pages:** `/black-friday`, `/pricing`, and the blog or newsletter of every major training, lab, tool and hardware vendor. Include vendors that skipped 2025.
- **Search:** queries like `"Black Friday 2026" cybersecurity course`, `pentest`, `OSCP`, `SANS`, `bug bounty`, `hacking lab`, `threat intelligence`, `Flipper`/`hak5` hardware, `password manager`, `VPN` (only security-focused).
- **Other deal repos** (linked in `README.md`): scan for security entries not in our list, then verify them at the vendor's site before adding.
- **Communities:** r/netsec, r/cybersecurity, r/AskNetsec, r/oscp, Discord/Slack of training vendors, Mastodon/X/LinkedIn infosec accounts, and security newsletters.
- **Past years:** `2018.md` to `2024.md` list vendors that skipped 2025 but may be back.
- **Submissions:** PRs and issues on this repo.

Search by role to avoid blind spots: pentest, red/blue/purple team, AppSec/DevSecOps, DFIR, threat intel/OSINT, GRC, awareness, cloud security, reverse engineering/malware, hardware hacking, careers/certs.

Add a deal only if it has a source link, a code or clear instructions, dates if known, and is infosec relevant.
